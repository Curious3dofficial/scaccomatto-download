A proper analysis board

Set up a position or paste in a game, and Argus, the engine we wrote from scratch for Scaccomatto, shows you the best moves and who's ahead. You can explore openings, build your own repertoire and drill it until it sticks.

If you'd like a nudge during a game, you can switch on the evaluation bar or ask for a hint. You can also pick between three piece sets, change the board colours, and play in light or dark mode.

Getting it running

Windows

  1. Open the Scaccomatto-Free-Setup file you downloaded and follow the steps.
  2. If a blue box says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. It shows up because the game is new and the installer isn't signed with a paid certificate.
  3. Open Scaccomatto Free from the Start menu or your desktop.

Updating? If the installer asks you to change, repair or remove, remove the old version first, then run it again.

Linux

  1. Download the .deb file.
  2. Open a terminal in the folder you saved it to and run this: sudo apt install ./scaccomatto-free_1.1.0_amd64.deb
  3. Open Scaccomatto Free from your app menu.

To remove it later: sudo apt remove scaccomatto-free

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no account and nothing to buy inside it. The full version, with online play and Game Review, will be a separate game on Steam and itch.io.

Can I play people over the internet?

Not in this version. Online play is part of the full version, which is coming soon.

What about Mac?

Not yet. Windows and Linux come first.

Does it send anything about me anywhere?

No. Scaccomatto Free never goes online, and everything it saves stays on your computer. The privacy page has the details.

Something broke. Where do I tell you?

Email alekhinestudios@gmail.com. If the game saved a problem report (Settings, then About, then Problem reports), attaching it helps a lot.
