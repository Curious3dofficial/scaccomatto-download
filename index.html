Seven ways to play

Classic, Chess960, King of the Hill, Three-check, Atomic, Fog of War and Duck Chess. Sit a friend down at your computer and pick one.

Bots with a backstory

Every bot has a name, a story and a way of playing. Gary will grab anything you leave lying around; Margot, at 2200, is a different story. Each one has its own strengths and blind spots.

Watch the bots fight it out

Pick any two bots, sit back, and watch them play each other. Good for learning, or just for watching Gary suffer.

Analysis, built in

Set up any position, or paste in a game, and Argus shows you the best lines and who's better. There's an opening explorer, and you can build your own repertoire and drill it. Turn on the evaluation bar or a best-move hint during your games if you want a nudge.

Installing

Windows

  1. Run Scaccomatto-Free-Setup and follow the steps.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because the installer is new and isn't signed with a paid certificate yet.
  3. Open Scaccomatto Free from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.

Updating? Remove the old version first if the installer asks you to Change, Repair or Remove.

Linux

  1. Download the .deb file.
  2. In a terminal, in the folder you saved it to:
    sudo apt install ./scaccomatto-free_*_amd64.deb
  3. Open Scaccomatto Free from your app menu.

To remove it later: sudo apt remove scaccomatto-free

No. Everything stays on your computer. The privacy page has the details.
