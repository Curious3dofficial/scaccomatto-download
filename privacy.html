Privacy

Last updated November 2026. This covers Scaccomatto Free 1.1.0.

The short version: Scaccomatto Free never connects to the internet. It has no accounts, it doesn't track you, and it doesn't send anything about you to us or to anyone else. Everything it saves stays on your computer.

What the game keeps on your computer

  • Your settings.
  • Any player names and pictures you add. These live in a folder called .scaccomatto in your home folder.
  • Problem reports, if something goes wrong. They're saved in .scaccomatto/logs and never leave your computer unless you decide to send one to us.

What we don't do

  • There are no accounts, so we never ask for an email address or a password.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected in the first place.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may log your IP address for security, as its own privacy statement explains. The fonts come from Google Fonts, and the download buttons ask GitHub which installer is the newest, so both of them see your IP address the way any website you visit does. The site itself doesn't store anything about you.

Removing everything

Uninstall Scaccomatto Free, then delete the .scaccomatto folder in your home folder. That's all of it.
